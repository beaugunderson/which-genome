How to compare
  • Individual credit-card purchases only. Provider-arranged doctor consultations are allowed; outside-referral-only, quote-only, business and study services are excluded.
  • Depth is not breadth or a quality score. Exome tests target coding regions; low-pass sequencing often uses imputation.
  • Price groups stay alphabetic by native currency in either sort direction; no FX conversion. First-year obligatory fees are included, not lifetime costs. Dante sorts by its USD base option. SelfDecode’s dollar currency is unconfirmed. Shipping, tax and eligibility can change totals.
  • Unknown sort values stay last; ties retain source order. Numeric ranges use the lower comparable value. Weeks convert to seven days and months to thirty; working days, conflicting estimates and maximum-only deadlines have no numeric timing key.
  • BAM and CRAM are distinct alignment files; VCF/gVCF are variants and FASTQ contains reads. Announced downloads are not available-file promises.
  • CAP accreditation, CLIA certification and ISO standards differ. A wellness coach, doctor or consultant is not necessarily a genetic counselor. Evidence limits and named-lab claims are in each expansion and the audit.
  • No purchases or sequence-quality tests were performed. Retail access does not establish fulfillment. Missing evidence does not prove closure. Please do not share personal genetic data in corrections.

# Consumer WGS/WES source audit — 2026-09-08

## Current scope and evidence standard

**This supersedes the 2026-09-07 broad clinical/research directory.** The public comparison now has **26 product/assay rows from 20 retail providers**. Report-only variants of the same assay are compacted, not counted as separate tests. Provider counts are sellers, not independent laboratories. Older useful primary-source research remains in [the historical audit](2026-09-07.md), prominently marked superseded.

Include human WGS/WES an individual can buy with a credit card at a public fixed price. **Provider-arranged consultation/prescription is allowed**, including the user's clarification that a quick doctor chat is worth listing with its step and fee. Exclude outside-referral-only, B2B, institutional, study enrollment, quote-only and prelaunch/waitlist services. Ordinary account, consent and shipping steps are not institutional barriers. No public pending or defunct directory.

A current product plus explicit self-service purchase route, price and terms can establish retail access without an independently rendered final payment page. **Browser/HTTP blocks alone do not disqualify an established retail offer.** This is why Sequencing.com and SelfDecode remain, unlike Banksia's actual coming-soon destination. A working checkout does not prove fulfillment or clinical quality. No purchases, account registration, personal-data entry, provider contacts, consent attestations or final payment/order submissions were performed. Browser navigation can initialize anonymous carts/hosted payment sessions automatically; those are not completed orders.

**Later field corrections:** see [Additional gap-fill](#additional-gap-fill); that section supersedes specified fields without erasing the initial observations below.

### Dates, methods and limits

All observations below were accessed **2026-09-08**, using primary pages, public HTML/JSON/application sources and installed Playwright CLI (Chromium). No source publication date is assumed from a copyright year, search snippet or generic dateModified. Where no dated publication is given, the page is **undated; accessed 2026-09-08**. Explicit older technical documents remain identified as older, not represented as newly revalidated assay performance.

Normal browser product → cart/order navigation recovered multiple pages that raw extractors called blank/403. No anti-bot bypass, location spoofing, provider contact or login was attempted. Browser evidence logs and screenshots are retained outside the repository at `/tmp/consumer-browser/` for independent local review. Exact stable purchase URLs are below; generated session URLs are recorded by destination pattern rather than publishing reusable session tokens. The local raw evidence contains the observed full URLs. Technical specifications remain provider claims; no sequence benchmark, fulfillment cohort or accreditation-registry check was done.

### Important adjudications for maintainers

- **CircleDNA Premium is included**: the official explicit WES technical article, current same-product NGS description and verified retail purchase support inclusion. The omission of “WES” from newer copy, “carefully selected regions,” and 3M versus 31M data-point counts do not prove a changed assay. Do not equate bases, markers and variants. Current capture details/depth are not independently established.
- **Human Longevity is included with a real PCP condition**: contract requires an existing PCP or establishing primary care at commencement and sharing PCP contact details. It does not require an outside prescription in the reviewed terms. Do not recast this as simply an included quick consultation.
- **Veritas myGenome is included with provider prescribing consultation**: its advertised post-purchase consultation is credible direct evidence. The standard card cart's required Prescribing Doctor field remains an operational caveat, not permission to fabricate a doctor or claim an uninterrupted self-referral checkout was completed.
- **Sequencing.com and SelfDecode are included from explicit primary retail evidence**, with browser limitations below, not a public pending label.

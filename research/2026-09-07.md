## Scope and evidence standard

As-of/access date: **2026-09-07**. This refresh reviews human germline WGS/WES personal, genealogy, clinical and research offers. It is broad discovery, not an exhaustive global hospital or every billing-SKU catalog. “New” below means missing from the previous site, not a verified launch date. Reporting/speed bundles may share the same underlying assay; supplier and reseller are not independent laboratories.

Evidence consists of primary provider product, FAQ, terms, catalog and regulatory-filing passages inspected during research. Dynamic pages sometimes required raw HTML, JSON-LD or official application UI strings. We do not promote search snippets or automated source-classifier verdicts to primary verification. Quotations below are short extracts; source language and context govern. Undated pages are not assumed newly updated because of copyright years. A source's claim is not an independent performance result.

No orders, logins, submissions, fulfillment cohort, downloaded sequence benchmark, laboratory-registry verification or checkout totals were tested. Automated access failures do not prove closure. “Offer observed” means a public offer/order route was observed; “service observed” means product/inquiry/catalog evidence, not acceptance of every patient. Partial and dated evidence is explicitly labeled. Unknown does not mean no, and a generic corporate credential/HQ does not establish the performing lab.

## Reading the table and maintaining sort keys

Native currency only. Price keys represent advertised upfront prices (including labeled starting/promo/schema prices), never lifetime totals. Required renewals and optional fees remain visible. Alphabetic currency groups AUD/EUR/INR/USD stay fixed in either direction; unknown currency or amount sorts last. Bare dollars at SelfDecode, Genome Computer, ODIN and Human Longevity were not verified with currency codes; prices remain visible but have no numeric price key. They must not be pooled as a single assumed currency.

Every data cell has an explicit `data-sort`; empty means no sortable value, not zero. Numeric columns are depth, price, turnaround and read length. Sort within access sections, leaving pending records in source order. Comparable ranges use their lower stated bound: e.g. 4–6 weeks → 28 calendar days, 5–7 months → 150 approximate days, 100 or 150 bp → 100. Business/working-day timings, multi-configuration values, blended assays and ambiguous depths have empty keys rather than false precision. WES and WGS depths sort numerically only as labeled metrics, not quality scores. Stable ties use source order.

Alignment cells explicitly distinguish BAM from CRAM; VCF/gVCF and genotype exports are not raw reads. CAP/CLIA and ISO standards are separate claims, all registry-unverified here. Wellness coaching, generic consultants and AI are not automatically genetic counseling.
